Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about rooftop solar installation, grid net-metering, government subsidies, and payback periods.

How does net-metering work with my local DISCOM? +
A bi-directional net meter replaces your standard electricity meter. During daytime, surplus solar electricity produced by your roof is exported to the DISCOM grid. At night, you draw power back. Your monthly bill is generated only for the net units consumed.
How much subsidy can I receive under PM Surya Ghar Yojana? +
Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential households receive ₹30,000 for 1kW, ₹60,000 for 2kW, and a maximum capped subsidy of ₹78,000 for systems 3kW and above. SolarNova manages the end-to-end portal filing for direct bank credit.
What is the average payback period for a home rooftop solar plant? +
The typical payback period ranges between 3.5 and 4.5 years depending on your monthly slab rate. After payback, the remaining 20+ years of solar generation deliver pure free electricity.
Do solar panels generate power during cloudy or rainy days? +
Yes. Modern N-Type TOPCon panels capture ambient diffuse sunlight and continue generating between 25% and 45% of peak capacity even on overcast monsoon days.
