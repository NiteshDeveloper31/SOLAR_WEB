How We Work

4-Step Execution Workflow

01. Roof Audit

Engineer site visit, 3D shading simulation & Structural Load Testing.

02. DISCOM Sanction

Filing load sanction application & obtaining CEIG safety approvals.

03. Installation

Rapid 7-day mounting of Tier-1 TOPCon panels & smart string inverters.

04. Net Metering

Meter installation, grid synchronization & PM Surya Ghar subsidy credit.
