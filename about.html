Our Mission

Powering India's Clean Energy Revolution

Founded in 2011, SolarNova Energy has grown from a specialized solar engineering boutique into one of India's most trusted EPC developers, commissioning over 145 MW of solar capacity across 21 states.

Pioneering Solar Engineering

We combine advanced 3D drone roof modeling, N-Type TOPCon bifacial cell architecture, and automated DISCOM net-metering workflows to deliver maximum lifetime kWh output.

Every project is designed for 25-year structural integrity, backed by 24/7 cloud telemetry asset management.
