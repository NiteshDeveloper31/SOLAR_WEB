Commercial Rooftop Solar Plants

Transform empty commercial roofs into financial powerhouses. Slash operating overheads, shield your business from grid tariff hikes, and claim corporate ESG tax credits.

40% Accelerated Depreciation

Under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act, commercial enterprises can write off 40% of the solar asset value in Year 1, dramatically lowering corporate tax liabilities.
